# Recall OS: The Truth Engine

**Recall OS** is a diagnostic learning platform designed to eliminate self-deception in competitive exam preparation. Unlike traditional flashcards or notes, Recall OS uses a variance-phrasing engine to verify if you actually understand a concept or if you've simply memorized the shape of a question.

## The Core Problem: The Illusion of Competence
Most students confuse "exposure" with "retention." Reading a concept five times feels like mastery, but when faced with a slightly different framing in an exam, the recall fails. Recall OS is built to break this cycle.

## Key Features

### 1. Framing Variance Engine
Every concept in Recall OS has multiple framing variants. The session logic picks a random concept and then a random framing of that concept (Direct, Application-based, or Inverse). This ensures that your brain retrieves the *core logic*, not just a specific word sequence.

### 2. The Illusion Meter
A real-time diagnostic that compares your perceived confidence (from onboarding) with your actual verified recall competence. It tells you exactly how much you are lying to yourself about your readiness.

### 3. Topic Stability Heatmap
Visualizes the decay of your knowledge across different topics. It doesn't just show if you're "good" at a topic; it shows if your mastery is stable or if it's currently decaying.

### 4. Truth Protocol
A strict, minimalist interface designed to induce "Active Recall" without the crutch of notes or hints.
